## Decided
- Tier: EQ.
- Royce's call, given a corrected picture: build the proper event-driven fix, not another retry-timeout widen. Made **after** a live Sentry query (this session) overturned the earlier same-day "track only, preview-only" triage — 7 of EQ-FIELD-10's 26 events are production, spanning ~20 releases, including one real `sks`-tenant hit.

## Deferred

This fix makes that failure faster and correctly diagnosed in Sentry going forward, but doesn't close it — that needs a product decision (give `eq` a backend? SEED-based map data instead? a demo-specific message?), logged in `eq/pending.md`, not defaulted on.
