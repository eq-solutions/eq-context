## Notes
- **EQ Field's leave/roster architecture has fully diverged from SKS** — not something to port between the two apps going forward. SKS still writes approved leave into `schedule` cells and now has to reverse that on delete. EQ Field removed the write-back entirely in v3.5.281/282 (PR #433, 2026-07-10): `leave_requests` is the single source of truth, and the roster/calendar overlay it live at render time — a delete needs nothing reversed. If SKS ever merges into EQ Field, SKS's write-back model is the one that should die. Recorded in memory (`project-sks-eqfield-sync.md`).
- **The EQ Field Calendar gap was a real, live, previously-untracked regression** — not something flagged in `eq/pending.md` or the digest before this session. PR #433 updated `roster.js`/`dashboard.js` for the new overlay model but missed `calendar.js`; anything approved after 2026-07-10 silently stopped appearing on the main Calendar page (Roster grid and the Leave tab's own mini-calendar were unaffected, since they don't share that code path). General lesson for future migrations of this shape: grep for every other consumer of the assumption being changed, not just the pages the PR's author happened to be looking at.

All work done in isolated worktrees (`sks-nsw-labour`: existing dedicated worktree for this session; `eq-field`: fresh `calendar-leave-overlay` worktree off `origin/main`, removed after merge).
