- **Real gotcha, hit twice this session, worth knowing for any future `@eq-solutions/*` version bump on a `github:`-pinned dependency:** `npm install` silently no-ops on re-resolving a git dependency when package.json and package-lock.json's *top-level* manifest declarations already agree — even if the deeper resolved commit in the lockfile is stale. ## Notes
- **Real a11y bug caught by axe during MultiSelect development, worth remembering as a pattern:** `role="listbox"` + `aria-multiselectable="true"` requires `role="option"` children — native `<input type="checkbox">`/`<label>` pairs (which are already self-describing to screen readers on their own) are not valid children of that role and axe correctly flagged it as a critical violation. Fixed to `role="group"` with an `aria-label`, the correct ARIA shape for "a popover containing a checkbox group." Any future popover-with-native-form-controls in this library should reach for `role="group"` or no special role at all, not `listbox`/`menu`, unless the children are actually built as `option`/`menuitem` elements.
