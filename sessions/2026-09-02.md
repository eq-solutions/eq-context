## Notes
- **Claude Code's own permission classifier hard-blocks this repo's `main`** for this kind of session — not just `git push`, but branch-creation-toward-main too (hit both, confirmed twice). - **Root cause of several "I ran it, nothing happened" reports in a row**: `GIT_EDITOR=true git ...` is bash syntax.
